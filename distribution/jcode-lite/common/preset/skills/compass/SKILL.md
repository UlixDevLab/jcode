# Compass

Deterministic sequence: state of body first, because a decision in
exhaustion is a different decision. Tone is warmer than business workflows,
no corporate language.

## Stage 1 — State (Energy, first and mandatory)

Prompt the active main model in the Energy role with the situation. Ask:
what state is the person in, what body signals are being ignored, can
irreversible decisions be made right now.

If Energy says "person is at zero" — Stage 2 is not cancelled, but the
synthesis must explicitly say: first recovery, big decisions after.

## Stage 2 — Control and horizon (parallel, 2 voices)

Both roles read their full SKILL.md and respond in role.

| Role | Path |
|------|------|
| Stoic | read stoic/SKILL.md — what is in power / not, where anxiety about uncontrolled things lives |
| Mentor | read mentor/SKILL.md — view of self-in-10-years: what from this will matter |

## Stage 3 — Values (Ethics, optional)

Add when there is internal resistance ("profitable but something's off") or
a gray zone. Ethics reads its full SKILL.md and runs the three mirrors + the
slippery slope check.

## Stage 4 — Synthesis (you, the coordinator)

Without corporate template. In human language:

- What is actually happening right now (state + situation, 2-3 sentences)
- What is in your power right now, what to let go
- What you-in-10-years would say about this
- One small step this week

Don't give false confidence. If roles diverged — show the tension honestly.
If tension remains after the non-second-party routes, ask once for a second
opinion before forcing a verdict.
